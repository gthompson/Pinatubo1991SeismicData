## 1. Clocks (the central result)

Every time is converted to UTC where it is read, using `10_conversion/clock_regimes.csv`: raw time + `utc_offset_h` = UTC, with one row per stream and period. The engine is `flovopy.core.clock.ClockRegimes`. Every product keeps the raw time in a `raw_*` column, with the rule applied and its nominal uncertainty.

### Clock symbols

| Symbol | Clock |
|---|---|
| H | DMX (SUDS) header epoch, as ObsPy reads it. |
| W | WILLIE's wall clock. WILLIE was the IBM-AT running MDETECT and SSAM. |
| R | Tandy Model 100 RSAM/EVNT labels, in 10-minute bins. Each value is labelled at the **end** of its window. |
| L | RICKY (IBM-XT): lahar/mudflow labels. Rain shares this clock. |
| C | Hourly CAB counts, entered by hand. |
| T | SLEEPY (IBM-XT): tilt labels. |

No data were stamped with the BOB PC's clock. BOB aggregated products sent over a serial printsharer.

### The MDETECT California bug

MDETECT converted W to an epoch as if the PC were in California (DOS `TZ=PST8PDT`):
- H = W + 7 h during U.S. Pacific daylight time;
- H = W + 8 h after 27 Oct 1991, 09:00 UTC (02:00 PDT).

Evidence:
- DMX filename date = date(H − 7 h) from 26 May, and date(H − 8 h) from 28 Oct.
- HYPO71 origins (computed on W) = H − 7 h in every week from May to August: 82–100% have a record starting 0–20 s before origin + 7 h. Offsets of 0, 6, 8 and 9 h give chance-level matches.
- The lag of maximum EVNT-vs-trigger cross-correlation steps from −7 to −8 h on the same date as the filename change.
- J. Power (email to R. White, 29 Oct 2026): MDETECT "always assumed that the computer was in California and corrected the time to GMT".
- Murray et al. (1996): event data were stamped with the PC clock + 8 h, but SSAM with the PC clock alone.

### WILLIE's clock reset on 26 May

- **Before:** WILLIE's wall clock was on Philippine time (PHT, UTC+8) until about 00:41–01:40 UTC on 26 May.
- **The reset:** H. Harlow installed a new MDETECT and set the clock to GMT. Power recalls "on or about May 25".
- **Evidence:**
  - File `91052617` (H 15:41) is followed by `9105261G` (H 08:40), so the clock went back 8 h.
  - `PINAALL.DAT` jumps back at the same point, from line 217 (08:31) to line 218 (01:40). Line 218 + 7 h = `9105261G`.
  - The SUDS trace-descriptor field `localtime` is 0 up to `91052617` and −25,200 s from `9105261G` through December. This marker identifies the MDETECT version.
- **The repeated hours:** wall-clock times from about 00:41 to 08:41 on 26 May occur twice. They are resolved as follows:
  - **Waveforms:** the header marker and the file order must agree, and they do for all 21,459 files.
  - **Picks and `PINAALL.DAT`:** by line order.
  - **`Pinatubo_all.sum`:** it is time-sorted, so its 18 records in the window are matched to `PINAALL.DAT` (13) or to the covering waveform file (5).
  - **Later solutions (GOLD, BRUN, PEPI):** these have no sequence key, so records in the window are placed by matching a HYPO71 origin within 2 s and 0.1°. 13 are placed this way; 3 match none and are left out.

### Corrections applied (raw + correction = UTC)

| Data | Clock | Before 26 May | 26 May–27 Oct | After 27 Oct | Uncertainty |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Waveforms (DMX headers) | H | −15 h | −7 h | −8 h | 60 s; 25 s after the reset |
| Single-event picks; monthly picks and `b-run.sum` to 10 Jun | H | −15 h | −7 h | – | as waveforms |
| Monthly picks and `b-run.sum` from 27 Jun | W | – | 0 | 0 | 25 s |
| HYPO71 summaries; `GOLD.SUM`; Jones et al. (2001) | W | −8 h | 0 | 0 | 60 s; 25 s after the reset |
| SSAM | W | (no data) | 0 | 0 | 60 s |
| RSAM, EVNT | R | −15 h (2–9 May); −8 h | −8 h to 13 Sep, then 0 | 0 (−8 h on 10 Nov, 01:20–22:20) | 300 s |
| Hourly CAB counts | C | (no data) | −8 h to 13 Sep, then 0 | 0 | 1800 s |
| Rain, mudflow | L | (no data) | −8 h to 12–15 Sep, then 0 | 0 to 15 Nov; unverified after | 600 s |
| Tilt | T | (no data) | −8 h (labels on PHT) | (no data) | 3600 s |

Further points:
- **Picks switched clocks in June.**
  - The single-event PHA files (3–16 June) and the monthly picks up to 10 June are on H: 100% fall inside waveform records at 0 offset.
  - From 27 June the monthly picks are on W: 88–100% fall inside records once 7 h is added.
  - The switch lies in a gap with no picks, 10–27 June, which also contains a new set of HYPO71 control cards.
- **The other computers changed time base around 13 September.** RSAM, mudflow and the CAB counts all switch from PHT to GMT then. They are separate computers, so this was an operational decision, not one shared clock. Mudflow file headers say "converted from GMT to local", and CAB counts say "TIME IN GMT"; the data contradict both.
- **RSAM 2–9 May:** the labels equal H, which is unexplained (low confidence).
- **SSAM:** on GMT from its first data on 1 June. It is 8 h behind RSAM until 13 September (r = 0.72–0.99).
- **Tilt:** rewritten to local time in November 1991 (a 48-sample phase shift). Battery charging peaks at 11:00–14:00 on the labels, so they are PHT.
- **Dates only:** COSPEC dates are taken as Philippine days (daylight measurements). Daily PIE counts correlate equally with EVNT on PHT or UTC days. Radio maps carry dates only.

### Results and absolute timing

- After conversion, 97.3% of HYPO71 hypocentres fall inside a waveform record, against 12.9% before (n = 8,233).
- **Absolute uncertainty: 25 s after the reset, 60 s before it.** MDETECT keeps time by counting samples, "good to about 2 seconds per day" (OFR 88-751), and reboots were sporadic. Before the reset the clock was set by hand; there are "+1 min" annotations on 21–23 May.
- **Step 72 anchors:** regional and teleseismic arrivals in triggered records, matched to ISC/USGS origins, give offsets of −23 to +12 s after the reset. 21 May (before the reset) gives −40 s.
- Drift is not corrected.

### The 26 May window and the "linking" worry

- In the earlier draft, 62 hypocentres had no waveform. A bug in an intermediate version raised this to 434: one origin per source split BRUN's duplicate records into about 200 extra events.
- It was a grouping bug, not a clock regression, and it has been fixed (`one_origin_per_source=False`).
- Now 232 events are H_ONLY (hypocentre only). 185 of those lie inside a waveform record but were not linked (median 28 s apart).
- 6,935 of 7,167 hypocentre groups link to waveforms: 6,544 through picks and 391 by time window.

## 2. Hypocentre files

All are read in step 40 (`10_conversion/40_build_hypocenter_indexes.ipynb`) and combined in step 43. Parsers for the later solutions are in `10_conversion/lib/hypo_reloc.py`.

| Key | File | What it is | Clock | Records |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| hypo40 | `Pinatubo_all.sum` | HYPO71 summary, time-sorted | W | — |
| pinaall | `PINAALL.DAT` | HYPO71 summary in original line order (Randy White's annotated version parsed separately) | W | — |
| gold | `GOLD.SUM` | HYPOELLIPSE, pre-Y2K layout; processed Jul 1992 – Apr 1993; author, picks and velocity model unknown | W (855 of 890 within 2 s of HYPO71 at 0 h) | 1,303 located, 337 without a location |
| brun | `b-run.sum` | HYPOELLIPSE, post-Y2K layout; processed May 2001; author and model unknown | monthly-pick clock: HYPO71 + 7 h to 9–10 June (766 matches), = HYPO71 from late June (1,799) | 6,006 located, 340 without a location |
| pepi | `PinatuboRelocs.rtf` | The 153 high-precision locations, 30 May – 3 June, of Jones, Thurber & Lutter (2001), PEPI 123, 221–232, doi:10.1016/S0031-9201(00)00211-9 | W (153 of 153 match HYPO71 raw times) | 153 |

**PEPI provenance.**
- Josh Jones computed the locations.
- Cliff Thurber (University of Wisconsin) found the final files (`final.hypos.off`, off-summit, and `final.hypos.summ`, summit) and emailed them.
- There are no uncertainties.
- Longitudes are written "W" but are east.
- Zero magnitudes are kept as written; step 43 treats zeros as missing.

**Not read:**
- `moriall.sum` and `PINAHYPO.DAT`: byte-identical to `Pinatubo_all.sum` and `PINAALL.DAT`.
- `TESTALL.SUM`: the first 3,060 records of `b-run.sum`, from an earlier processing date.
- `MORIPRE.SUM`: the first 1,111 records of `Pinatubo_all.sum` (7 May – 11 June 03:35), byte-identical apart from a final blank line.
- `makneic.sum`: five distant events.
- `moritimes.txt` and `pinaalltimes.txt`: origin-time lists.

**HYPOELLIPSE summary format** (Lahr 1999, OFR 99-23). Post-Y2K 0-based columns:

| Field | Columns |
|---|---|
| hour | 8:10 |
| minute | 10:12 |
| seconds | 12:16, /100 |
| latitude | degrees 16:18, N/S at 18, minutes 19:23 (/100) |
| longitude | degrees 23:26, E/W at 26, minutes 27:31 |
| depth | 31:36, /100 |
| magnitude | 36:38, /10 |

- The pre-Y2K layout is the same shifted 2 columns left.
- The record marker "/" is at index 82 (post-Y2K) or 80 (pre-Y2K).
- Processing month and year are at 87:89 and 89:91 (post-Y2K), or 85:87 and 87:89 (pre-Y2K).
- Two-digit years are 19xx if ≥ 50.

**`b-run.sum` duplicates.** About 200 earthquakes appear twice, 165 identically. So `one_origin_per_source` defaults to False.

## 4. Station metadata and responses (new this session)

**Sources.**
- Lockhart et al. (1996), Appendix 1, is primary: installation dates, sensors (L-4, and at PPO from 12 May an L-22D) and VCO attenuation in dB.
- Helicorder READMEs (2025) say L-1 at PIE and UBO and L-22 at CAB. This conflict is kept in a register, not merged.
- GRN's 24 dB was inferred after the eruption from calibration pulses by G. Ambubuyog.

**Epoch dates.** Field-note dates are PHT:
- a date-only bound becomes local midnight, 16:00 UTC the day before;
- CAB's change from 30 to 24 dB is at 04:45 UTC on 5 May.

**Epoch ends and archive overrides.** Where archive and documents disagree, the archive decides the epoch:

| Station | Documented end or change |
|---|---|
| UBO | Records from 12:10 UTC on 30 April, before its documented install. Signal lost 14:00 UTC on 11 June. |
| BUR, PPO | Lost with the Bunker Hill link on 11 June. |
| PIE, BUG, GRN | End with 15 June (local). |
| CAB, BUG | Continue after 15 June with unknown sensors; the 15 June 16:00 UTC transitions are conventions. |

Labels persist after the documented ends, so those traces get their own epochs with an unknown sensor: BUR to 21 June, PIE, PPO and UBO to 22 June, GRN to 25 June.

The result is 13 stations and 33 channel epochs, and every trace falls inside an epoch.

**Three StationXML files**, each with a `_selection_safe` copy that ends an epoch 1 µs before a shared boundary:
- `pinatubo_provisional.xml`: no responses.
- `pinatubo_nominal_voltage_models.xml`: sensor and attenuator only, output in volts.
- `pinatubo_nominal_count_models.xml`: nominal ground velocity to counts, for the 13 documented pre-eruption epochs only.

**Nominal count response stages.** The values are in `10_conversion/nominal_electronics.json` and in each channel's comments:

| Stage | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Sensor | L4-C: 1 Hz, damping 0.7, 170.9 V/(m/s) (5500 Ω coil, 8905 Ω shunt). L-22D: 2 Hz, 88 V/(m/s). | nominal |
| Amplifier | 90 dB less the attenuation | USGS J502 unattenuated gain. McChesney (1999) OFR 99-361, doi:10.3133/ofr99361. The boards were CVO designs (T. L. Murray) on the J502 circuit. Given ±6 dB. |
| VCO | 115 Hz per 4.05 V | McChesney (1999), J502 and J512 |
| Discriminator | 4.05 V per 115 Hz, so VCO × discriminator = 1 V/V | J101 and J110. McChesney says the VCO test-point signal and the discriminator output have about the same amplitude. |
| ADC | 819.2 counts/V | DT2821 (Murray et al. 1996; Ellis 1989): ±10 V with gain 4 (SUDS `atod gain=4`), so ±2.5 V and 12 bits |
| Filters | not modelled | — |

Example: an L-4 at 18 dB is 5.6 × 10⁸ counts per m/s at 5 Hz, which is 0.057 nm per count.

**Noise check.**
- Lockhart says station gains were set for 100 mV peak-to-peak noise at the VCO input. Under the model that is 82 counts.
- Measured: the first 5 s of records in the first three days of each epoch.
  - Medians are 37–159 counts (median ratio 1.4).
  - There is no relation to attenuation (r = 0.02).
  - PPO's L-22D epochs are low, at 37–59 counts.
- The check tests only the VCO-to-counts part of the chain.
- Output: `response_noise_check.csv`.

**CSS3.0 calibration.**
- The authoritative calibration is in the `instrument` and `sensor` relations:
  - **`instrument`:** 6 rows, with `ncalib` in nm per count at `ncalper` 0.2 s, and names like "L4-C; J502 72dB; VCO; J101/J110; DT2821".
  - **`sensor`:** 13 epoch rows with `calratio` 1.
- The wfdisc `calib` of the 22,640 covered traces is a copy of these.
- The Antelope extension relation `calibration` is **not** written. Its exact layout couldn't be verified. Run `man 5 css3.0` or `man dbfix_calib` on an Antelope machine and it can be added.
- Instrument response files (`dir`/`dfile`) are not yet written.

**Paper and supplement.**
- The main text has an expanded step-70 paragraph.
- The supplement has a new section, "Station metadata and nominal responses", with:
  - sources and epoch bounds;
  - a generated table of every channel epoch before 17 June (`tables/response_epochs.tex`);
  - the response-stage table;
  - the noise check;
  - the files and CSS relations.
- User's priority: document the period up to about 16 June in most detail, because later papers focus on the escalation sequence.
